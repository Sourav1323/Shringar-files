FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a slot on Shringar?
+

Go to any design page and click "Add to Cart". Once you have chosen all the designs you want, go to your Cart and click "Proceed to Slot Booking". You can then choose your date, preferred time slot, and artist. Fill in your details and pay the 30% advance to confirm.

Can I cancel my booking after confirmation?
+

Yes! You can cancel for free while your cancellation timer is active. Your booking details page displays a live countdown timer showing the exact hours left to cancel for free. The earlier in advance you book, the more hours you get to cancel for a 100% full refund of your advance. Once the hours-left timer reaches 0, the cancellation button is removed to protect your artist's schedule.

Can I reschedule my booking?
+

Yes, you can reschedule your date while your cancellation timer is active directly from your Order Details page. Your advance payment carries over with zero additional fees.

What if the artist is late or doesn't arrive?
+

Our artists are committed to punctuality. If an artist is delayed by more than 30 minutes without prior notice, you are entitled to a ₹200 discount on your balance. If an artist cannot make it, you receive a 100% full refund. Please raise an urgent concern below so our management can take immediate action.

Is home service available?
+

Yes! All our bookings are home-service by default. Your artist arrives at the address you provide during checkout. We currently serve Mumbai, Pune and Nashik. An artist visit fee of ₹200 is added to your order, regardless of location within the city.

How do I raise a concern or report an issue?
+

You can use the "Raise a Concern" section directly on this page. Choose your concern category, enter your booking reference, and describe your issue. Our admin team reviews all tickets promptly and posts official responses right into your account.

How do I provide feedback at the end of service?
+

At the end of your appointment (or when your service is marked complete), a feedback card automatically appears on your Booking Details and My Bookings page. You can give a 1–5 star rating and comments. Your feedback is sent directly to the Admin Feedback Inbox to help us reward top artists and ensure quality.

Why is only 30% charged in advance?
+

We believe in a fair model for both customers and artists. The 30% advance secures your slot and confirms the artist's visit. The remaining 70% is paid directly to the artist after your design is completed to your satisfaction. This ensures quality accountability.

What payment methods are accepted?
+

We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) with zero convenience fee, plus major cards and net banking. The remaining balance after your mehendi is completed can be paid via cash or UPI directly to the artist.

How long does a refund take?
+

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days to your original payment method. You'll receive a notification once the refund is initiated.

Is the advance refundable if cancelled?
+

Yes, 100% of your advance is refundable if cancelled while your live countdown timer is active (hours left to cancel). Once the timer ends (0 hours left), cancellation closes and the advance is non-refundable because your artist has already blocked their time and prepared natural cones.

How does the AI Try-On feature work?
+

Our AI uses computer vision to detect your hand or foot in a photo, identify your skin tone, and overlay the selected mehendi design in a realistic way. It adjusts the color depth and pattern opacity to match what the actual design would look like on your skin. Upload a clear photo in good lighting for the best result.

Is my photo stored or shared?
+

No. Your uploaded photo is processed in real time and is not stored on our servers. It is not shared with artists or any third party. We take your privacy very seriously and comply fully with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act.

What devices support the AI Try-On?
+

The AI Try-On works on all modern smartphones and desktop browsers. For the best experience, use Chrome or Safari on a device with a good camera. The feature requires an active internet connection.

Can I request a custom design?
+

Absolutely! Use the "Special Requests" field during booking to describe your custom design, or send us a reference image on WhatsApp. Our artist will discuss the design with you on arrival. Custom designs are priced based on complexity and are confirmed before your appointment.

What does the cone quantity mean?
+

Each design listing shows approximately how many 25g mehendi cones are used. This is an estimate. The artist brings all materials — you do not need to arrange cones separately. The cost is included in the design price.

Can I mix multiple designs in one booking?
+

Yes! That's exactly how our cart works. Add a hand design, a feet design, a back-hand design — whatever combination you want. The total duration and cost will be calculated automatically. Your artist will apply all selected designs in sequence.

How do I get the darkest colour from my mehendi?
+

For the darkest stain, follow these tips:

  • Leave the mehendi paste on for at least 6–8 hours (overnight is best)
  • Apply a mixture of lemon juice and sugar over the dried paste to keep it moist
  • Avoid water contact for at least 12 hours after removing the paste
  • Apply mustard oil or a balm to seal in the colour after scraping off the paste
  • Expose your hand to gentle warmth (not direct flame) to deepen the stain
How long does mehendi last?
+

Natural mehendi typically lasts 2–3 weeks on hands and 3–4 weeks on feet. The colour deepens over the first 24–48 hours and then gradually fades. Avoid prolonged soaking in water, harsh soaps and chlorine pools to extend the life of your design.

Is the mehendi 100% natural?
+

All our artists use 100% natural henna — pure henna powder mixed with essential oils like eucalyptus or lavender. We strictly prohibit the use of black henna or any chemical PPD additives, which can cause skin reactions. If you have sensitive skin, please mention this in your special requests.

How are artists verified on Shringar?
+

Every artist goes through a 3-step verification: portfolio review, in-person skill test by our team, and background verification. Artists must maintain a minimum rating of 4.5★ to remain on the platform. All artists are re-evaluated every 6 months.

Can I request a specific artist?
+

Yes! During slot booking, you can choose your preferred artist from those available on your selected date and time. Artist availability is shown in real time. If your preferred artist is unavailable, you can choose another or pick a different time slot.

What if I'm not satisfied with the design?
+

Your satisfaction is our priority. If you are unhappy with the design during the session, please raise the concern immediately so the artist can adjust. If you have a genuine quality complaint after the session, contact us within 24 hours with photos and we will investigate and offer a partial refund or a complimentary redo session.
